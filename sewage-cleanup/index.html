Few emergencies in a building are as hazardous as sewage backing up into it. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. Encinitas Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout Encinitas, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Water damage comes in categories, and sewage sits in the worst one: Category 3, called black water, the highest level of contamination and danger. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. Our technicians follow IICRC S500 standards for Category 3 water damage so your property is restored to safe occupancy standards.

Here is what the work covers: standing sewage is pumped out, contaminated water is extracted from the structure, porous materials the sewage touched (drywall, insulation, carpet and padding) are torn out and disposed of properly, hard surfaces are disinfected thoroughly, antimicrobials are applied, and the structure is dried so mold does not follow. Lingering sewage odor is eliminated with thermal fogging and industrial hydroxyl generators, which we run in the affected area.

How to Prevent Sewage Cleanup Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • A drain that is slowing down should be dealt with early, while it is still partly open
  • Food waste, oils and grease block pipes, so put them in the trash and not the sink
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
  • Wipes clog sewer lines even when the label says flushable, so only toilet paper goes down the toilet
  • Keep downspouts and sump pump discharge out of the sanitary sewer so it does not overload in heavy rain
  • Have roots removed where trees grow by the sewer, before they get inside the pipe and plug it

When to Call for Sewage Cleanup in Encinitas

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • A rotten egg or sewer smell from the drains or the lowest floor of the home
  • Drain openings or basement slabs coated with visible sewage residue
  • Gurgling sounds from toilets or drains when other fixtures are used
  • People in the household getting stomach or intestinal symptoms with no clear cause
  • A cleanout that weeps sewage, or wet foul patches in the lawn along the sewer path
  • Multiple drains in your home backing up at the same time
  • Carpet or flooring contaminated when a toilet overflowed with solid waste

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

This is one cleanup you should never take on yourself. Because raw sewage is a biological hazard, the work demands trained people, protective equipment (gloves, boots, respirators and Tyvek suits) and disinfection products registered with the EPA and not sold to the public. Hepatitis, gastrointestinal disease and infections of the skin or respiratory system are among the serious illnesses these pathogens cause.

Professional sewage cleanup also requires proper disposal of contaminated materials as biohazard waste. It is illegal to throw sewage-contaminated insulation, drywall or carpet in the regular trash, and doing so puts the community and the workers who collect it at risk. Certified restoration companies maintain proper waste disposal protocols and relationships with licensed biohazard waste disposal facilities.
